Separate Campaign Type From Mechanism

Current dropdown mixes occasion, promotion style, scoring rule, and delivery workflow. Splitting them makes future campaigns easier.

Campaign Type - what kind of activity is this?
🎊

Festive / Seasonal Gift

Raya, CNY, Deepavali, Christmas. Usually gift/delivery focused.

🎁

Free Sample / FOC

Debtor list with exact package, or category-based sample plan.

📣

Brand Promotion / Offer

Push brand sales, penetration, or segmented offers.

🏆

PK / Competition

Agent or group ranking, pool, winners.

Example: Festive Gift Campaign
Campaign Type
Festive / Seasonal Gift - Raya
Mechanisms
1. Gift Entitlement: eligible debtor list
2. Delivery Tracking: claim / delivered / verified
3. Optional Purchase Condition: only if bought selected item this month
Sales Dashboard shows gift package and delivery action. Management sees delivery progress and audit.

Recommended Admin Flow

Step 1 choose Campaign Type for label/defaults. Step 2 add one or more Mechanism Cards. This supports festive gifts, BISON segmented offers, IFACE conversion, and TR-style PK without hardcoding everything into one dropdown.
